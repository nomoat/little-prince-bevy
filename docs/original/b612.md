# Chapter 1 — B612 (original design analysis, analyst A)

Scene `B612_Main`. Main classes: `B612.B612Story` (Assembly-CSharp), `B612Opening`, `B612ClocheRitual`,
`B612RoseActing`, `B612PlanetParams`/`B612LayoutData`, `B612PlanetBoundary`, `B612ExitGate`, `B612Birds`,
`B612.Rendering.DayNightCycleSettings`/`SunCycleSettings`, plus shared `CarryProp`, `FillTarget`,
`WaterTarget`, `CarryPutSpot`, `ChairSeat` (see `shared-core.md`).
Text is paraphrased; the original Chinese lines are not reproduced here.

## 1. Chapter card and objectives — OBSERVED (`B612Story` static ctor, resolved string refs)

- Number: chapter 1. Subtitle meaning: "the last day on B-612". Narration (2 lines): a planet barely bigger
  than a house; a little prince lives on it.
- Event 1 "look after the rose", steps: (1) fetch the watering can from the tool shed, (2) fill it at the
  spring, (3) water her one last time.
- Event 2 "watch the last sunset", step: go and sit on the chair at the planet's edge.
- Free-roam line before the events start: "walk around and get to know the planet" (`FreeLine` const).
- Journey goal type for this planet: `JourneyGoal.B612Sunset` (departure opens only after the sunset). The
  refusal text "watch a sunset first, then depart" exists (string 12276) — INFERRED to be the departure refusal.

## 2. World layout — OBSERVED field names/tooltips, values partly INFERRED

The planet is a **domed terrain** (sphere-cap heightfield, world −Y gravity), not a sphere.
`B612PlanetParams` (tooltips): cap radius "50–60 m, drop 4–5 m at the walkable edge", optional quartic
flattening, walkable disc radius, soft push ring, failsafe ring, a rounded lip into a rock rim/underside,
gentle noise. A cloud sea below the rim (`cloudSeaY`, `cloudSeaRadius`).
- **Boundary** (`B612PlanetBoundary` ctor, OBSERVED): soft radius **21.9 m**, hard radius **23.2 m**; push
  toward centre from 0.25 m/s up to 5 m/s at the hard radius ("jog is 2.4, run 3.5").
- **Exit gate** (`B612ExitGate`, OBSERVED): half width 2.3 m, depth 3.8 m, soft depth 2.2 m, push 0.35–4.2 m/s
  (keeps the player out of the parked departure spot `exit`).
- Sites (`B612PlanetSite` = x, z, yaw, size), all OBSERVED as fields:
  - `nook` — sleeping nook: a dug pit with a stone ring, sleeping pad (bedScale), exit ramp toward home;
    spawn faces east toward the sunrise.
  - `shed` — tool shed (open front); the watering can starts here, empty.
  - `rose` — the rose on a low mossy mound on a knoll (`roseKnoll`, visible over the crown from the nook);
    rose ≈0.65–0.75 m tall (`roseScale` 1.39 on a 0.54 m prefab, then `roseShrink`), a small **wind screen**
    north-north-west of her (scale 0.7, ~0.75 m tall), a **big flat-topped boulder** next to her holding the
    **glass cloche** (glass 0.46 m across × 0.65 m at scale 1; `clocheScale` 0.75 so it covers her with ≥2 cm).
    A can put-down spot beside her (`canSpotBearing/Distance`).
  - `chair` — sunset chair near the edge; seated facing west (sun sets at game azimuth 180.6°).
  - `pond` — spring pool with a sand-boil vent (`B612WaterParams`), a stone ring with an overflow notch,
    a step-pool brook running to the rim and a waterfall off the edge. Pool surface y = 3.84 (`FillTarget.waterLevel`).
    Fetch slabs: one main slab + 3 more at compass bearings N, E, SSW ("filling at one slab only was fiddly",
    playtest 2026-10-11).
  - `craterC` dormant crater ring + `volcanoA`, `volcanoB` active cones (procedural, foot ≈2.48× height) —
    **three volcanoes**, matching the book. Steam plumes / vent glow (`B612SteamPlume`, `B612VentGlow`).
  - `seedlings` — baobab sprouts (placeholders). No interaction or prompt string found → decoration (INFERRED).
  - `path` from the nook opening to the chair (sunk 3–5 cm).
- Ambient life: butterflies, pollen, leaves (`B612AmbientLife`), geese/birds (`B612Birds`), wind, clouds.
- Review cameras (names only): Entry, Exit_LookBack, Meadow_Stream, Rose_Main, Rose_Side, Stream_Fetch,
  Sunset_Seated, Sunset_Stand, Volcano_Low, Volcano_Pass.

Suggested Bevy scale (INFERRED from the above): walkable radius ~22 m, rose ~8–10 m from spawn, shed and spring
on opposite sides, chair at ~18–20 m on the west edge.

## 3. Beats in order

### 3.1 Opening: asleep → wake up → camera orbit (OBSERVED `B612Story.Start`, `B612Opening`)
1. Phase `card`. Chapter card (3 s) plays **over the sleeping prince** (`sleepSeconds` = **4.5 s** asleep).
2. Wake-up clip at speed **1.2**; camera one rises with the clip (between `riseFrom`–`riseTo` of the clip,
   about 0.3–0.95, INFERRED from ctor values).
3. Camera two orbits from behind to his front (eased; extra arc over the pit rim at mid orbit), front view looking
   past him at the rose's cloche and the shed, then hands over to the player camera. `CameraPath` field names are
   OBSERVED; the values (orbit ≈5.2 s, frontYawOffset 9°, pitch ≈4°, distance ≈3.4 m, FOV ≈38°, orbitArc ≈6°,
   frontHold ≈1.4 s, handOverTime ≈1.6 s) are INFERRED from a partly ambiguous ctor decode. Stages:
   None/Asleep/WakingUp/Standing/Orbiting/Front/Done.
4. Phase `explore`. Free line "walk around…".

### 3.2 Controls tutorial (OBSERVED `TeachControls` + `<TeachControls>g__Step`)
After 1.2 s, three steps, each a key-cap card + text; each completes when the summed input reaches a need
value, or after **25 s** (step timeout constant):
1. keys W A S D — "move" — need **1.5** (seconds of move input, INFERRED unit);
2. mouse — "click the view, then move the mouse to look around" — need **150** (look degrees, INFERRED);
3. Shift — "run" — need **1.0** (seconds running).
Also taught somewhere: "press once to see the chapter goal / press again to hide" (string 12309 group).
After the last step, `guideAfterTutorial` = **0.8 s**, then `GuideToRose`: the objective "go and see the rose"
and a **stardust trail** to her.

### 3.3 The rose calls (OBSERVED `B612Story.Update` + `RoseCalls`)
- Trigger: in phase `explore`, the prince comes within `roseCallDistance` = **4 m** of the rose.
- Phase `call`. Teaching UI hidden, target cleared. If he carries something he is refused/held.
- He is walked to `callKeep` = **1.3 m** from her at `walkInSpeed` = **1.1 m/s**.
- One medium shot from behind him toward her: pitch 14°, distance 2.6 m, FOV 40°, 30° off the line
  (`callPitch/Distance/Fov/SideAngle`), checked for clearance on both sides.
- Rose pose `Lift`; two rose lines: (a) she hints it is time for her breakfast; (b) she asks him, a bit
  coquettishly, to kindly think of her. Then pose `Rest`, camera released (0.3 s).
- Phase `rose`. `MarkDone`, and the stardust trail shows for **7 s** toward the next target.

### 3.4 Care loop: can → spring → water (OBSERVED `Update`, `FillTarget`, `WaterTarget`, `CarryProp`)
Target selection each frame (`Update`):
- can still in the shed → step 1, star target "watering can";
- can carried but not full → step 2, target "spring" (nearest fetch slab);
- can full → step 3, target "rose".
Interactions (verbs from `Start` setup, OBSERVED): can "pick up can" / "put can back" (home put-back within 1.2 m);
spring "fill"; rose "water"; when the can is empty the rose shows "the can is empty" (no key); cloche
"lift the glass" / "put it back"; put-spot "put the can down beside the flower"; chair "sit down".
- **Fill** (`FillTarget`): offered within 1.8 m outside the pool waterline when facing the pool centre within
  60° (+ half the pool's angular span); the prince is walked to the nearest slab stand, crouches and dips the
  can (clip-driven; bubbles 45/s, ripples 5/s, drips 70/s; gurgle pitch 0.95→1.12). Camera: −70° yaw from the
  stand facing (his right, the can side), pitch 40°, 2.2 m, enter 1.1 s / exit 1.3 s. Result: `fillLevel` = 1.
- **Water** (`WaterTarget`): `requireFullCan` = true. Stand spot from the rose toward `Rose_CareStand`.
  `soakTime` = **1.8 s** of full flow soaks the soil (wet tint (0.55,0.52,0.5), smoothness 0.2→0.55).
  Watering empties the can and fires `Watered` → the farewell starts.

### 3.5 Farewell + glass cloche ritual (OBSERVED `Farewell` coroutine, `B612ClocheRitual`, `B612RoseActing`)
1. Phase `farewell`. If he still carries the can he is told to set it down beside her first (put spot);
   he then steps to `farewellKeep` = **0.75 m** from her and turns to her. Two-shot framing: distance 2.7 m,
   FOV 40°, camera height 1.0 m, look height 0.36 m, turned 15° toward his face; close-ups FOV 32°;
   glides of 1 s between framings (no cuts).
2. Dialogue part 1 (rose poses in brackets): prince says goodbye → [Droop, tremble 0.7 s] → rose silent
   ("…", 2.6 s) → prince says he wants to see other planets → [tremble] → stage direction: she coughs softly
   (1.8 s) → [Lift] rose: she was silly, asks forgiveness → prince silent ("…", 1.4 s) → rose: yes, she loves
   him; it was her fault he never noticed → [Rest] rose: but he was just as silly.
3. Camera eases back; the **glass ritual** is enabled (`HoldBegan` hook). The player presses E at the
   cloche/rose ("lift the glass"): he walks onto `Cloche_Stand` (0.9 m/s, ≤2.5 s, detours round the stones on
   rings of 16 points, keeps 0.26 m from her stem, ≤2 re-plans; any move input > 0.6 aborts), turns ≤1.6 s,
   lifts the cloche off the boulder with both hands, turns ~180° and holds it over the rose
   (stages Approaching/Turning/Aligning/Lifting/Holding/Placing). Camera goes round to his face:
   stand-frame cam (−0.8, 0.78, −2.05) look (0, 0.72, −0.22), FOV 38°, enter 2.4 s / exit 1.8 s.
4. Dialogue part 2 while he holds the glass: rose tells him to leave the glass, she does not want it →
   prince: but the wind… → [Proud] rose: the cool night air will do her good, she is a flower → prince: but the
   animals… → rose: she fears nothing, she has her claws → stage direction: she shows her four thorns (1.6 s)
   → [Droop] rose: don't linger, it's annoying → rose: he decided to leave, so go → [TurnAway], 0.8 s.
5. Put-back is now allowed: **hold E 1.2 s** (`placeHoldSeconds`) to set the glass back on its boulder → `Finished`.
6. Phase `sunset`. Target "chair". (If the soil is still wet > 0.2 he first turns to face her — `FaceRose`.)

Rose acting values (`B612RoseActing` ctor): idle sway 1.4° at 0.22 Hz, head sway 0.9°; Droop tilt 75° /
lean 12°; Lift tilt 9° / lean 3°; TurnAway yaw 40°, tilt 14°, lean 5°; Proud tilt 5°; pose time 0.6 s,
droop time 1.6 s; `Tremble(seconds=0.6, amplitude=3°, delay)`.

### 3.6 Chair line and the last sunset (OBSERVED `Update`, `Sunset`, day-cycle settings)
- Any time in `explore` (or before event 2 starts), coming within **2.4 m** of the chair once makes the prince
  say to himself that he likes sunsets very much (`_chairLine`).
- Day clock (`DayNightCycleSettings` ctor, OBSERVED; mode DayHold): the free day runs slowly
  (`dayWaitPace` 0.2) and **never reaches the evening by itself** — the sun creeps toward and rests above
  22° elevation (`dayWaitElevation`). **Sitting on the chair** hops time to the golden hour in **3.5 s**
  (`sitHopSeconds`) and plays the sunset to the dusk hold in **22 s** (`sitSunsetSeconds`); standing up
  mid-sunset keeps it running at 0.6× pace. Full loop (debug) 190 s: day 90 / dusk 22 / night 36 / dawn 24 /
  morning 18. Sun: latitude 55° N look, declination +2.555°, sets at azimuth 180.6° in the seated view.
- `Sunset` coroutine after the first sit (`FirstSunsetRequested`): target cleared → wait **5 s** → prince
  line: when one is very sad one likes sunsets (auto 5 s) → wait until the day-over flag, max **30 s** →
  Phase `depart`, chapter complete. `B612GameEvents.DayOver` afterwards; the chair then shows a
  "the sun has set for today, come back another day" prompt.
- Migrating birds (`B612Birds`) flock in for the departure (~3 s delay; calls every 2.5–5 s; day flyovers every
  70–150 s at 20–30 m). Departure prompt: Q to leave with the birds / Esc to stay.

## 4. Win / fail conditions
- Win: watered the rose (full can), finished the farewell + glass put-back, sat for the sunset → `depart`.
- No fail state. Soft guards (OBSERVED tooltips): cannot sit or use the cloche with the can in hand (hint);
  empty can cannot water (hint); departure refused before the sunset (INFERRED text use).
- Stall help after 40 s without progress toward the star target (shared).

## 5. Hidden goals / achievements
None found for B612 (no `SetHidden` call with a goal text; INFERRED). The volcanoes and baobab sprouts have no
interactions in the shipped code (INFERRED from absence of prompts/strings).

## 6. Camera / cinematic moments (summary)
Opening wake-up + orbit; rose call medium shot; fill-can over-shoulder shot; farewell two-shot glides and
close-ups; dome ritual shot round to his face; seated sunset view (chair view mode, ±40° look).
